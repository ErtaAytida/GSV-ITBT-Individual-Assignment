ABOUT SKYBRIDGE

An air cargo partner for fast-moving supply chains.

SkyBridge Air Cargo is a conceptual logistics service created for the ITBT live-class exercise. It connects businesses with air-freight solutions for urgent, valuable and time-sensitive cargo.

Our model combines airport handling, shipment documentation, cargo tracking and first/last-mile coordination into one straightforward service experience.
